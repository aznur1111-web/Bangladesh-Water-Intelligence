Device security baseline

Real devices should authenticate with per-device credentials, send data over TLS, carry device timestamps and calibration metadata, and never expose write endpoints to unauthenticated public users. Browser-added demo stations are not registered field instruments.

Recommendation policy

The system may identify and rank candidates for expert review. It must not command gate changes, river diversions or dredging without validated hydraulic models, environmental checks and approval by the responsible agency.

Data provenance rule

Every measurement should store its source, timestamp, units, sensor or laboratory method, calibration status, QC flag and whether it is observed, estimated or modeled.
